How the Super Rich Avoid Paying Taxes

Professor Scott Galloway breaks down how the wealthiest individuals and corporations use completely legal strategies to slash their tax bills. Borrowing against assets instead of selling them, relocating to zero income tax states, and taking advantage of Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS) under Section 1202. The biggest takeaway? The tax system rewards owners over earners, and the sooner you shift from earning a salary to owning equity, the faster your wealth compounds.

If You Want to Change the World, Start by Making Your Bed

Admiral William H. McRaven's 2014 commencement address at the University of Texas at Austin lays out 10 life lessons from Navy SEAL training. Start with small wins like making your bed. Find teammates because nobody changes the world alone. Measure people by the size of their heart, not their appearance. Accept that sometimes you'll fail despite doing everything right. Let hard experiences make you stronger. Take risks head first. Stand up to bullies. Stay calm in your darkest moments. Give others hope. And above all, never quit.

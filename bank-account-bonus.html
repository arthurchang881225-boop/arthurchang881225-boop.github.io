Needs direct deposit Needs a deposit you keep in the account
    Before you open an account
    What counts as direct deposit
    A paycheck, pension or government benefit sent by your employer or the government. Transfers you make yourself, Zelle and mobile check deposits usually don't count.
    New money
    Deposit bonuses need money from another bank. Moving money between accounts at the same bank doesn't count.
    Keep the balance until the end
    If the balance dips below the tier you're aiming for during the holding period, you can drop to a smaller bonus or lose it.
    Bonuses are taxed
    Banks report the bonus as interest income on a 1099-INT, so it's added to your taxable income for the year.
    Monthly fees
    Set up the fee waiver from day one, or the fees can eat into the bonus.
    Wait before closing
    Keep the account open until the bonus posts. Some banks take back the bonus or charge a fee if you close within a few months.
    Credit checks and ChexSystems
    Opening a bank account is usually a soft check that doesn't affect your credit score. Banks do check ChexSystems, and opening many accounts quickly can lead to a denial.

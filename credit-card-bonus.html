      Before you apply
      Chase 5/24 rule
      Chase usually declines you if you opened 5 or more personal cards from any bank in the past 24 months. Most business cards from other banks do not count.
      Chase 24-month bonus rule
      United, Southwest and IHG cards pay no bonus if you have the card now or earned its bonus in the past 24 months.
      Chase IHG family rule
      IHG Premier and the no-fee IHG card exclude people who hold any personal IHG card, or earned a personal IHG bonus in 24 months. Premier Select is separate.
      Amex lifetime rule
      You can usually earn a card's welcome bonus only once in your life.
      Amex family rule
      Having held the personal Platinum can block the Gold bonus, so open Gold first.
      Amex eligibility pop-up
      Amex warns you before approval if you will not get the bonus. Close the application when you see it and no hard pull happens.
      Amex 5-card limit
      Amex lets you hold about 5 Amex credit cards at once. Charge cards like Platinum, Gold and the business charge cards do not count toward it.
      Amex 2/90 rule
      Amex approves at most 2 new credit cards in 90 days.
      Citi 48-month rule
      No bonus if you earned a bonus on the same Citi card in the past 48 months.
      Citi 8/65 rule
      Wait 8 days between Citi applications, and no more than 2 in 65 days.
      BofA 2/3/4 rule
      Bank of America approves at most 2 new cards in 2 months, 3 in 12 months and 4 in 24 months.
      Barclays 6/24 caution
      Barclays often declines people with 6 or more new accounts in 24 months.
      Minimum spend reality check
      Only open a card whose minimum spend you can reach with spending you would do anyway. Interest charges erase the bonus quickly.
